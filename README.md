# Stranded: Mars Odyssey

You are a lone Mars rover operator stranded on the harsh Martian surface after a catastrophic sandstorm damaged your communication systems. With limited power reserves and time running out, you must navigate the unforgiving terrain, scavenge essential resources, and repair the signal tower before nightfall brings deadly freezing temperatures.

The red planet is littered with debris from previous missions - scrap metal, power cells, and advanced technology components scattered across the desolate landscape. Your survival depends on efficient resource management, careful navigation, and quick thinking as the day-night cycle threatens to drain your rover's precious power reserves.

Can you collect enough materials, restore the signal tower, and send a distress signal home before it's too late?

---

## HOW TO PLAY

### OBJECTIVE
Complete all mission objectives before time runs out:
1. **Collect Resources** - Gather 5 Scrap Metal pieces for repairs
2. **Find Power Cells** - Locate power sources to energize the signal tower
3. **Repair the Signal Tower** - Use scrap metal to fix the damaged communications array
4. **Activate the Tower** - Power up the tower with your rover's energy reserves
5. **Send Distress Signal** - Transmit your emergency beacon before nightfall

### CONTROLS

#### Movement
- **W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys** - Drive the rover
- **Mouse** - Look around (if camera control enabled)

#### Actions
- **Drive over collectible items** - Automatic pickup
- **TAB** - Open/Close Inventory
- **F** - Interact with Signal Tower (when in range)
- **ESC** - Pause Menu

#### Inventory Management
- **Left Click** - Select item in inventory
- **Consume Button** - Use consumable items (restores power/heat)
- **Discard Button** - Remove unwanted items
- Drag and drop items to organize your inventory grid

### GAME MECHANICS

#### Resource Management
Your rover has limited carrying capacity. Manage your inventory wisely:
- **Scrap Metal** - Used to repair the signal tower (need 5 pieces)
- **Power Cells** - Can be consumed to restore rover power
- **Tech Components** - Advanced technology for future objectives
- **Cooling Units** - Reduces heat buildup in your systems

#### Rover Attributes
Monitor your rover's vital systems:
- **Power** - Depletes over time and with movement. Collect power cells to recharge
- **Heat** - Increases with activity. Too much heat causes system damage
- **Speed** - Affected by terrain and rover condition
- **Cargo Capacity** - Limited inventory space (6x4 grid = 24 slots)
- **Communication Range** - Distance to signal tower (visible on HUD)

#### Day-Night Cycle
Time is your enemy:
- **Daytime** - Safer exploration, better visibility
- **Nighttime** - Extreme cold drains power faster, reduced visibility
- **Time Limit** - Complete objectives before the timer reaches zero
- Watch the day/night indicator on your HUD

#### Signal Tower Repair Process
1. **Locate the Tower** - Use the distance indicator on your HUD
2. **Collect 5 Scrap Metal** - Drive over scrap metal scattered across the map
3. **Approach the Tower** - Get within interaction range (you'll see a prompt)
4. **Press F to Repair** - Consumes 5 scrap metal from your inventory
5. **Ensure Sufficient Power** - Your rover needs 50+ power units
6. **Press F to Activate** - Transfers 50 power to the tower
7. **Mission Complete!** - Distress signal sent!

### COLLECTIBLE ITEMS

#### Materials
- **Scrap Metal** (Gray) - Essential for tower repairs
- **Metal Plates** - Additional structural materials

#### Energy
- **Power Cell** (Blue) - Restores 20 power when consumed
- **Fuel Canister** (Yellow) - Provides sustained power over time

#### Technology
- **Tech Component** (Cyan) - Advanced electronics
- **Communication Module** (Purple) - Signal boosting equipment

#### Cooling
- **Cooling Unit** (Light Blue) - Reduces rover heat when consumed

### TIPS & STRATEGIES

1. **Prioritize Scrap Metal** - You need 5 pieces before you can repair the tower
2. **Manage Power Carefully** - Don't waste power driving aimlessly. Plan your route
3. **Use Consumables Wisely** - Save power cells for emergencies
4. **Watch the Timer** - Keep an eye on remaining time
5. **Stay Near the Tower** - Once you have materials, don't wander too far
6. **Organize Inventory** - Keep important items easily accessible
7. **Don't Overheat** - Monitor heat levels, especially during intense activity
8. **Explore Efficiently** - Items spawn randomly, but clusters are common near debris sites

### HUD ELEMENTS

**Top-Left Corner:**
- Power Bar (Battery icon)
- Heat Gauge (Temperature icon)
- Speed Indicator
- Cargo Weight

**Top-Right Corner:**
- Remaining Time
- Day/Night Indicator
- Communication Range to Tower

**Bottom:**
- Objectives List (TAB to view details)
- Interaction Prompts (when near tower)

### FAILURE CONDITIONS

You will fail if:
- ⏱️ **Time runs out** before completing objectives
- 🔋 **Power depletes completely** (if enabled)
- 🌡️ **Rover overheats critically** (if enabled)

### VICTORY CONDITIONS

You win when:
- ✓ All required objectives are completed
- ✓ Signal tower is fully activated
- ✓ Distress beacon is transmitting

## ADVANCED INFORMATION

### Inventory System
- **Grid-Based:** 6 columns × 4 rows = 24 total slots
- **Weight Limit:** Each item has weight; exceeding capacity slows the rover
- **Item Stacking:** Some items stack, others take individual slots
- **Quick Actions:** Right-click items for quick consume/discard options

### Resource Conversion
When collecting items, resources are automatically added:
- Scrap Metal → Materials counter
- Power Cells → Power reserve (when consumed)
- Tech Components → Tech counter

### Tower States
The signal tower has three states:
1. **Broken (Red Light)** - Needs 5 scrap metal to repair
2. **Repaired (Yellow Light)** - Needs 50 power to activate
3. **Active (Green Light)** - Mission complete!

### Pause Menu Features
Press **ESC** to access:
- Resume Game
- Settings (adjust volume, graphics)
- Return to Main Menu
- Quit Game

### Performance Tips
- Adjust graphics quality in Settings if experiencing lag
- Lower particle effects if frame rate drops
- Enable V-Sync for smoother camera movement

3. **Collect Resources** → Drive over glowing items (they auto-collect)
4. **Check Progress** → Press TAB to see objectives and inventory

## TROUBLESHOOTING

**Q: Items aren't collecting when I drive over them**
A: Make sure your inventory isn't full and you're not overweight

**Q: Can't interact with tower**
A: Get closer (within 5-10 meters) and ensure you have required resources

**Q: Game is too hard/easy**
A: Difficulty scales with time limit and resource availability (adjustable by level designer)

**Q: Lost/Don't know where to go**
A: Check the tower distance indicator on your HUD, use TAB to review objectives

**Q: Controls not responding**
A: Make sure you're not in a menu (close inventory/pause menu)
